**Crucially: enemy creep count holds at 14 through tick 150.** Our home tower (charged by tick ~10 by the worker right next to the container) does not appear to auto-fire. Either tower auto-fire requires intent calls, or our tower never charged, or charged towers are passive — open question.

### Specific failure mechanics (hypothesis)

The squad's `contest-flag` play re-targets the closest non-our flag. Once the close neutral is captured (~tick 100), the next target is across the river in enemy territory (either the second neutral at the off-diagonal corner, or the enemy flag). The squad pushes there as a single sprawled formation (peak spread 61 at tick 100). The front of the squad reaches enemy creep range while the rear is still crossing the river. Front gets focused-fired. Healers can't reach the front in time. By the time the squad regroups, 9 are dead.

### v1 hypothesis

> **Stop after the close neutral. Hold at 2 flags.**

Capture the close neutral, then hold the squad on/near it. Don't push for the second neutral. This:

- Banks the 2-flag floor (= guaranteed non-loss at tick 2000).
- Avoids the catastrophic over-extension that costs 9 creeps for 1 flag.
- Sets up Phase 2/3 (river growth + tower charging) as the natural follow-on.

The smallest change that tests it: in `commander.mjs`, gate `assignContestFlag` for the main squad on a "we hold ≥2 flags?" check; if yes, switch the squad to `assignDefendFlag` on the captured neutral.

### Other observations from the data

Suggests one of our remaining creeps (likely a healer + ranger pair at the captured neutral) is enough to retake a flag against an idle enemy presence — but they get whittled down each cycle. The enemy creep count never drops below 12 in this band; they're not committing forces to recapture, just probing.

- **No `lost-all-my-flags` or `all-non-my-flags-captured` events fired** in any match. The bot's win-trigger logic may be wrong (these only fire when state hits exactly zero), or the match cuts off in the in-client console before these states are reached. Worth verifying the trigger code.

### What the data shows

**Key observation:** by tick 100 the enemy has *also* captured their close neutral. Both sides land at 2 flags simultaneously. Our v1 policy ("hold when myFlags ≥ 2") fires, but **we don't have a 2-flag advantage** — both sides are tied. Worse, the enemy squad is now closer to *our* home flag than we are to theirs (because they captured the neutral on our side of the map). They keep pushing while we anchor, and they capture one of our flags at tick 200. Our remaining 2 creeps can't recover.

In one outlier match (the SE-corner one) we held a 2-2 stalemate at 3 vs 12 alive for ~1000 ticks before finally losing the last 3 creeps and ending at 1-3. Showed that 3 cohesive creeps can hold a flag for a long time, but never enough to win.

### Why v1 failed

The hypothesis ("stop pushing past 2 flags") assumed v0's tick-200 catastrophe was caused by *us pushing for the second neutral*. The actual cause is more fundamental:

- The squad sprawls during the advance (peak spread 49 at tick 100 vs ~5-10 we'd want).
- Sprawled creeps walk individually into enemy fire.
- Healers can't reach the stragglers in time.
- We lose 6+ creeps in the 50-tick window between tick 100 and 150, regardless of the play we assigned.

v1 changed *what we target* but didn't change *how the squad moves*. The cohesion problem is upstream of the play decision.

### Bonus insight: matchmaker tier is harder

In v0 placement (rating 445), 4 of 5 opponents played passively or didn't capture their close neutral by tick 150. In the v1 ranked batch, **every single opponent** captured their close neutral by tick 100. Either we've climbed to a tier where opponents are more skilled, or the matchmaker is paying back our 3-2 placement with harder fights. Either way, the assumption "we can capture neutrals uncontested" is now wrong.

### Critical bug discovered: cohesion radius blocks advance

`mainSpread` sat at **4-5 across every match, every tick**. Never grew, never shrunk. The cause: starting positions span (1-8, 1-8) — a 7-tile box — so initial spread is 7. With `COHESION_RADIUS = 3`, the squad enters cohesion mode at tick 0 and never exits. Members walk toward the centroid, the centroid moves with them, spread plateaus at 4-5 forever, **the squad never advances**.

The 3 distinct match outcomes match this bug exactly:

- **Wins (Vllo2, Я)** — Enemy walked into our home tower's range. We sat at home; tower fire whittled them from 14 → 11 → 8 → 5 → 4 → 2. Once depleted, we walked over and captured neutrals → 3-1 → win. Match 10 vs Я is the textbook example: enemy `14 → 11 → 8 → 5 → 4 → 3 → 2` between tick 50 and 350 while we stood still.
- **Draws (`(*'楊'*)`, `6.62607015e-34`)** — Enemy *also* sat home. Both bots stuck on starting flags; 1-1-2 stalemate held to tick 2000; equal flag count = draw.
- **Losses (6 matches)** — Enemy advanced and captured both neutrals while we sat. Tick 100 reads 1-3-0; tick 2000 (or earlier) confirms loss.

### Confirmed positives

- **Cohesion enforcement works** when it engages — spread stays bounded, no isolated stragglers getting picked off. The tick 100→200 die-off from v0/v1 is gone.
- **Tower fire works.** Match 10 proves charged towers shred enemies in range without our involvement.
- **Hold-at-2-flags is sound when activated** — but it never activates because we never reach 2 flags.

### v3 hypothesis

> *Bump cohesion radius from 3 to 7 so initial squad spread (7) doesn't trigger cohesion mode immediately. Cohesion engages only when the squad genuinely sprawls (spread > 7), keeping the v0/v1 sprawl-prevention while letting us advance.*

Smallest possible diff vs v2: one constant + a parameter knob. Everything else (hold-at-2-flags, tower commands) preserved.

### The cohesion fix worked, mechanically

Look at the consistent opening across every match in v3:

| tick | mine | enemy | flags | spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | 14 | 14 | 1-1-2 | 6 |
| 100 | 14 | 14 | 1-1-2 | 6 |

We now (a) advance, (b) capture the close neutral by tick 150, (c) lose only 1-2 of our creeps doing it, and (d) **kill 6 of theirs in the same window**. The squad spreads to 7 during the advance, then compacts back to 3 after the capture. Compare to v2 which sat at spread 4-5 the whole match, and v0/v1 which sprawled to 49 and lost 9 creeps.

### But the rating still dropped, sharply

The cohesion + tower-fire combo is producing **strong mid-games** (tick 200-600 typically reads `12 vs 5-7, 2-1-1, sp:3` — material advantage and a winning flag count) but the bot **doesn't know how to convert the lead.** Two failure shapes show up across the losses:

1. **Long-match erosion (the worst losses).** Stable at 12 vs 5 for 500 ticks, then around tick 600-800 enemy creep count climbs back (12, 13, 14) and ours drops (10, 8, 6). We lose flags slowly. Hypothesis: **enemy is harvesting body parts from the river** while we sit at the captured neutral; their creeps grow stronger creep-for-creep, eventually overwhelming us in straight engagements. (The river spawns parts in the *middle*, neither neutral; whichever bot patrols it grows.)

2. **Fast losses (under 400 ticks).** Probably enemies that rush both neutrals before our advance lands, putting us at 1-3-0 by tick 200 with no path back. Cohesion mode held our squad together but not fast enough.

### Both of v3's wins were tick-out flag-count wins

`Mouli v8` (+8) and `Я v25` (+7) both ended at tick 2000 with 2-1-1 flags — meaning we held home + captured neutral + the enemy held only their home + 1 neutral was uncaptured. Tick-out tie-break said we had more flags. Neither win came from actually capturing a third flag. Our "hold-at-2-flags" policy genuinely caps us at 2.

### The strategic gap

v3 is now **mechanically sound** (squad moves cohesively, towers fire, flags get captured) but **strategically incomplete** in two specific ways:

- **No way to convert material advantage into flag advantage.** When we're at 12-vs-5 alive at tick 400 and the close neutral is captured, we should push for the second neutral. Instead the policy gates us at 2 flags forever, allowing the enemy to recover.
- **No river presence.** We cede the body-part economy to the enemy entirely. In long matches, this compounds against us.

### v4 candidate hypotheses

Three reasonable next moves, ordered by my prior on which moves the rating most:

**A. Conditional push: "advance for the 3rd flag when we have a clear material advantage."** Smallest change. When `myFlags >= 2` AND our creep count exceeds enemy's by ≥4, override the hold policy to contest the next non-our flag. Reverts to hold once material advantage closes.

**B. River harvesting.** A 1-2 creep "scavenger" sub-squad that patrols the river center for body parts. Grows our DPS over time. More complex; requires per-creep role override.

**C. Don't ship anything — watch replays first.** We've iterated three times without ever watching an in-client replay. The fast losses (Darth Rick 225t, 无所事事 295t) almost certainly have a tactical signature we'd see immediately on screen but can't see in tick logs. Pause iteration; investigate.

I lean (C) personally — we have 10 hours of bot work and 0 hours of replay study. But (A) is the cheapest forward step if you'd rather keep moving.

**The loose-cohesion change is the proximate cause.** With `looseAdvance:true`, convergence to centroid only fires when an enemy is within range 8 of any squad member. Cohesion fires → squad converges to centroid → leading members **retreat from the contact zone** to meet the rear. Leaders die isolated; squad collapses.
